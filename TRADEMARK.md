# Trademark Policy

The Apache-2.0 license covers the **source code** of Xazz. It does **not** grant
rights to the Xazz name or logo. This policy explains what you may and may not do
with the project's marks.

## Marks

- The name **"Xazz"** and the Xazz logo.
- Related word marks used by the project (for example "xazz-guardrail" for the
  reference model).

These are trademarks of the project author (`Jangseowoo`, `ax1s.xazz.com`). The
Apache-2.0 grant in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE) does not imply any trademark license.

## You may (no permission needed)

- Use the name to refer to the project truthfully: "built with Xazz", "an Xazz
  pipeline", "Xazz 0.4".
- Redistribute unmodified copies of the software under the name Xazz.
- Use the name in articles, talks, tutorials, and comparisons, as long as you do
  not imply endorsement.

## You may not (without written permission)

- Use the marks in a way that suggests your fork or product **is** the official
  Xazz, or is endorsed by the project.
- Use the marks in a company, product, or domain name in a way that is likely to
  confuse users about the source of the software.
- Modify the software and continue to call the result "Xazz" without marking it
  as a modified fork (for example, "Xazz — FooCorp fork").

## Forks

A fork may keep the name only while it is a faithful redistribution. If you
change behavior, add features, or ship it as part of a product, use your own name
and add a clear notice such as "Based on Xazz, an independent fork; not
affiliated with the Xazz project."

## Reporting misuse

Open an issue or contact the maintainer (see [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) for the
private channel). We prefer to resolve trademark questions by clarification
first.

## Relationship to the license

This policy governs names and branding only. It does not change, and is not a
condition of, the rights granted by Apache-2.0 for the source code. See
[`docs/design/licensing.md`](docs/design/licensing.md) for the licensing boundary.
